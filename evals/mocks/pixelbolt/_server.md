**The recipe format** (what `pixel_help` teaches and `pixel_validate` enforces): required `version: 1`, `name`, `width`, `height` (1 to 256) and `frames` (1 to 256). `name` and every frame, symbol and animation name start with a letter, then letters, digits, hyphens or underscores. `palette` maps keys to `#RGB`, `#RGBA`, `#RRGGBB`, `#RRGGBBAA` or `transparent`; keys may be longer than one character, but only single-character keys can be used in `grid` rows, and `.` and space mean transparent there. A frame is `{name, duration?, from?, ops?, layers?, pixels?}`: `duration` is 1 to 60000 ms (default 100), and `from` copies an earlier frame by name. Layers are `{name?, opacity?, x?, y?, ops?}`. `symbols` maps names to equal-width text rows of palette keys, which `stamp` draws. The ops are exactly these 17: `pixel` (x, y, color), `rect` and `ellipse` (x, y, w, h, color, filled?), `line` (x, y, x2, y2, color), `clear`, `fill`, `replace`, `grid` (x, y, rows), `stamp` (x, y, symbol), `text`, `copy`, `outline`, `dither`, `shade`, `rewrite`, `autotile` and `tilemap`; any other `op` is an error such as `{"error":"project.frames[0].ops[0].op: unknown operation \"circle\"","path":"project.frames[0].ops[0].op"}`. `animations` maps names to `{frames: [frame names], direction?, loop?}`. Unknown fields are errors. An fx source is `{format: "pixelforge-fx", version: 1, name, width, height, palette, symbols, effects: {<effect name>: {...}}}`, where `symbols` is required (the named particle shapes its emitters draw; without it the error is `fx.symbols: expected named symbols for the particles to draw`). A fluid source is the same with `format: "pixelforge-fluid"` (smoke, fire, explosions, magic and water), except that its `width` and `height` are 4 to 128 and `symbols` is optional and names obstacle shapes. In both, `effects` is keyed by effect name.
