Tilda carries the tide post between the harbour steps and the ferry. She's a little puffin in a mulberry post cap, with a coral-and-butter bill, webbed feet and a sea-glass canvas satchel on a leather sling. The folded letter rides in its outer pocket. Keep the small carry loop readable and the soft bag close to the body as her feet and wings take short opposing steps.

Use the supplied standing art for the facing and palette. The down and right walks should feel like the same jaunty harbour waddle, at the existing beat and ground point.
