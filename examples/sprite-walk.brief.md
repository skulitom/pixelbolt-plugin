# Sprite walk: art brief

An existing side-view drawing turned into a walk and a quiet idle. The brown-haired character wears a teal tunic, dark trousers and brown boots, facing right. The drawing is our own, made for this example.

- **Canvas and display:** 32 × 32, origin `[16, 30]`. The ankle markers use `rig.ground: 29`; the boot soles sit on row 30. Review at 1× and 4×. `sprite-walk.png` is the original PNG, unchanged; the exported `stand` matches all 1,024 RGBA pixels.
- **Construction:** grids cut from the drawing: head, torso, near and far upper arms, forearms, thighs, shins and boots. A transparent 2 × 2 mass carries transparent two-bone limbs. The motion templates require these structural parts; they cannot use the grids directly as their body or limbs. `Y` and `Z` form the invisible ramp. Grids attach at the bones' start or end; `angle: -90` cancels the standing carrier angle. The boots stay upright.
- **Rest and walking stance:** `stand` preserves the drawing's separated legs. `walk-rest`, the template rig's rest, centres both hips at x = 16 with identical angles; all eight walk poses use that common hip. The contact poses put the ankles 8 px apart on both steps. Keeping the original stand separate avoids moving the source pixels to repair the gait.
- **Joins:** hidden tunic and neck colours fill behind the arms and chin. An extra thigh row and an overlapping shin row cover the rotating knees. `cleanup: false` on the small limb grids keeps their drawn outline pixels, including `(12, 26)` during idle. The torso's last trouser-coloured row is concealed at rest and bridges the belt when it rises. The source frame's colours supply all these fills.
- **Palette (20 visible colours):** `a` outline; `b c d e` hair; `f g h i` skin; `j k l m` tunic; `q r s` trousers; `n o p t` boots. Far limbs use darker colours. No new visible colours are introduced.
- **Motion:** `walk` uses the template defaults: stride 10, lift 2, bob 1, speed 24 px/s, eight 80 ms frames. `idle` has four 400 ms frames. Its authored overrides come from the idle template on `stand`, with `breath: 0` and the torso in the head role so its one-pixel bob carries the head and shoulders together; the downward exhale is replaced with rest. Three idle frames equal the PNG; inhale lifts the upper body 1 px. The left animations override every generated pose with a whole-pose mirror. They preserve the right animation's pixels and timing exactly.
- **Weaknesses:** carriers move rigid cut-outs; they do not skin or bend the drawing. Short shins and overlapping knees still look blocky, the outline changes thickness as parts rotate, and the flat boots have no heel/toe roll. The idle has only two distinct drawings, with a held rest rather than continuous breathing or cloth deformation. The walk's upper body moves only 1 px, and passing poses overlap the legs heavily. Front and back need new drawings and hand-keyed poses; the side templates do not produce them. Passing the motion checks does not remove these limitations.
